# Additional causal controls

Protocol fixed before generating the new schedules or examining their audited exposure metrics. The existing A/B, repeat, and C/D/F0/F48 studies retain their estimands, artifacts and analyses. This is a separate study of ordering policies on the approved V4 records. No training or inference is authorized by this document.

## Unit and interventions

Use the frozen teacher-first/trace-key order within each question. A block is its next at most four contiguous records in that canonical order. Long questions are split into fixed consecutive chunks, their chunks may be separated in the global order, and two chunks of the same question cannot be adjacent. All arms use exactly the same chunks. This makes the question-block control group-blind while respecting the repository's maximum run length of four. It differs from shuffling whole questions when a question has more than four records.

- **S0T0** is a NumPy group-blind shuffle. Draw one random priority per chunk, then emit the eligible chunk with lowest priority, respecting precedence and the adjacency rule. Retry only if the precedence/adjacency constraints strand chunks. This specifies the sampling law and does not imply uniformity over feasible schedules.
- **S1T0** balances true semantic exposure by decile while targeting the realized S0T0 supervision exposure.
- **S0T1** balances supervision (full_response/json_only) exposure by decile while targeting the realized S0T0 semantic exposure.
- **S1T1** balances both exposures by decile.
- **Placebo** uses the same objective as S1T0 after a seeded permutation of whole question labels within strata with the same canonical supervision sequence and chunk layout. Thus placebo label frequencies and their joint counts with supervision and block size are preserved exactly. The true question labels and records remain canonical. Record label reassignment rates and placebo manipulation fidelity. Placebo balance is a mechanism diagnostic, not a guaranteed no-effect control.
- **Low-gap and high-gap** start from S1T1 and rearrange within deciles, preserving all joint counts and token totals by construction. Optimize opposite directions of an equally weighted objective combining the mean largest no-exposure interval across eligible coarse groups (at least 50 records) and the coarse switching fraction. Intervals include the start and end of the epoch. Compare achieved gaps and switching jointly. This contrast cannot isolate their separate effects.

## Exposure definitions and eligibility gates

Use deciles `floor(step_index * 10 / 1984)`, with zero-based steps. Count both record and exact supervised-token mass by decile, with the token ledger produced by the frozen TRL collator. Full_response and json_only are the original loss scopes. Compute LR-weighted supervised token mass with the original pre-update linear LR; it is a schedule measure, not a gradient attribution. Report residuals, attained balance, and all failed gates for every seed. A failed schedule remains exploratory. There is no tuning on evaluation data.

For the 2×2, semantic balance uses both coarse and fine record counts. Supervision balance uses both scope record counts and scope loss tokens. For categorical counts, TV is half the L1 distance between each decile's composition and the reference composition, computed on the same decile, then averaged unweighted over deciles. For scope loss tokens use the same per-decile scope composition. Report maximum TV and component residuals as well as mean TV. Targets: corpus-wide composition when balancing is on, the realized group-blind profile when off.

- Semantic manipulation in each supervision stratum requires ≥0.025 mean coarse TV and ≥0.025 mean fine TV improvement versus the corpus-wide composition. Supervision manipulation in each semantic stratum requires ≥0.015 mean scope-count TV and ≥0.015 mean scope-token TV improvement.
- For supervision contrasts, match coarse and fine record-count compositions between arms by mean TV ≤0.035 and max TV ≤0.085 for each resolution. For semantic contrasts, match scope composition by mean TV ≤0.025 and max TV ≤0.065 for both counts and supervised tokens. These tolerate finite block granularity, and their residuals are reported. Scope×semantic conditional balances and LR exposures are descriptive in the factorial, not additional gates.
- Placebo eligibility requires at least 50% of records to be assigned a different fine label, placebo coarse and fine mean TV improvements ≥0.025 vs S0T0 under the same permuted labels, and scope matching at the same tolerances as the semantic contrasts. Report true semantic imbalance for placebo separately.
- Spacing pair requires equality by decile for every joint fine×scope record count, loss-token total and input-token total (therefore also marginal and coarse totals). Across the epoch, compare LR-weighted loss-token shares relative to the low-gap schedule's total mass. The absolute relative difference in the total must be ≤0.01 and TV-equivalent half-L1 changes ≤0.010 for coarse, ≤0.018 for fine, and ≤0.008 for scope. Also compare joint fine×scope LR-weighted mass by decile (half-L1 normalized by the epoch total ≤0.020). High minus low must be at least 20 steps in mean group maximum gap and at least 0.025 lower in coarse switching fraction. The best bounded candidates are retained as exploratory if the pair fails.
- Search limits and objective weights are fixed in the generator. Use training metadata only. The GPU allocation, decision criteria and results of earlier experiments are outside this new schedule search.

## Execution and inference

Ordering seeds are 1102, 211102, 2026113, 2026117, 2026131, 51001, 51002, 51003. Cross them with training seeds 43 and 51004. Use the same training seed for all paired arms. The training seed changes model stochasticity, not the precomputed chunk order. Confirmatory analysis requires all eight order blocks and both training seeds for a contrast; failed exposure or run gates make the contrast ineligible. Neither evaluation items nor intermediate checkpoints are independent training replicates.

Primary outcome is 100% checkpoint CT10K eligible fine-group Collapse20, using the frozen base and original strict >20pp threshold. The six two-sided contrasts are the marginal semantic effect, marginal supervision effect, their difference-in-differences, S1T0 versus placebo, placebo versus S0T0, and high-gap versus low-gap. Compute each linear contrast within paired training runs, average the two training seeds within each order block, then use exact two-sided order-block sign flips under symmetry and Holm family-wise 0.05 control across six planned tests. Ineligible tests remain in the family at p=1. Report block values and nominal 95% order-block t intervals with df=7 for eligible contrasts. With 8 blocks, inference remains coarse and depends on the symmetry assumption. Historical A/B contrasts stay outside this family. CT10K/H-dev are development sets and H-final is sealed.

Store checkpoint adapters at 496, 992, 1488 and 1984 optimizer steps using the frozen SaveAt callback. Evaluate the same eligible groups, fixed items, decoding and evaluation GPU at all four checkpoints only after the full training run. Report group correctness, maximum prior checkpoint accuracy minus final accuracy, final minus first accuracy, and per-arm run-level summaries. These secondary dynamics are descriptive, with no checkpoint, seed, or arm selection. Use the same receipt rules and frozen scorer for every checkpoint.

Record base/adapter/checkpoint identity, train/order/ledger SHA, GPU UUID, training library versions, scorer, prompt, serving image/library versions, sampling, and item IDs. Reuse historical data as descriptive context where fields differ or are missing. Do not pool historical data with new runs for intervention uncertainty. Before training, require current task-specific GPU authorization, a clean commit-pinned worktree, repository device and data gates, and a frozen order manifest. Preserve adapter binaries in external storage. No new GPU work in the CPU preparation.
